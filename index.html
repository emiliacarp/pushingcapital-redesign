Proposed redesign

Five directions, one set of words.

Every option below is the same website — same structure, same typography, same palette, and the copy is taken word for word from pushingcapital.org. Nothing has been rewritten, added or cut. What changes between them is the film behind the headline.

A and B share the same footage and differ only in layout — left-aligned versus centred. C, D and E are different films. E also flips the copy to the right, because that is where its footage goes dark. Pick a film and a layout independently.
A · Control room

Global operations floor

A curved wall of live route maps above tiered operator desks. The most literal read of “strategic operating intelligence” — this is the cockpit the software is.

Vehicle movement at night

E · The objects, connected

Everything a deal is made of

A brake rotor, a bearing, a key, a stamped title, carbon-copy forms and a fan of banknotes — the physical stuff of a deal — turning together as one cluster, with fine lines drawn between them and pulses running along the links. The objects are the business; the network is the software. The film sits in its own panel so the copy keeps solid ground.

The client surface at pushingsecurity.pushingcap.com is a full product — an encrypted vault, a Golden Record, P the concierge, live deal status and a catalogue of 89 services and plans. Nothing explains any of it to anyone who is not already signed in. So this one is a new site with new copy throughout, written from the product.

pushingSecurity

The secure client surface

Every capability and price on the page was taken from the live app. Nothing is claimed about certification, uptime or customer numbers, because none of it could be verified. Same type and palette as the pushingCapital pages, with the accent shifted to gold so the two read as one house rather than one site.
