Why it exists

Nobody loses a deal on the paperwork. They lose it on the wait.

The file sits in six inboxes. The lender wants one more statement. Nobody can say what is outstanding without asking three people, and by the time the answer comes back the window has moved. pushingSecurity closes that gap: one record, one place to see it, and one thing you can ask at any hour.
